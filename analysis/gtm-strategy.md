# Go-To-Market Strategy

## 1. Lender Persona Playbook

### PSB (Public Sector Bank)
- **Pain**: PSL shortfall penalties, INR 15K acquisition cost, branch can't reach last mile
- **Pitch**: "Auto-fill your PSL agriculture gap with pre-underwritten, compliant loans at 0 acquisition cost and <4% NPA"
- **Pricing**: INR 3-5L/mo platform + INR 200-400/loan
- **Pilot**: 500 dairy farmer loans in Gujarat, 90 days, shadow 30d -> production
- **Target**: CGM Priority Sector, DGM Agriculture, CTO digital lending

### Agri NBFC
- **Pain**: Can't cost-effectively serve small/marginal farmers, seasonal mismatch causes NPAs
- **Pitch**: "Extend to 8 agricultural sub-segments with segment-specific scoring and seasonal dynamic products"
- **Pricing**: Revenue share 2-3% or INR 1-2L/mo + per-loan
- **Pilot**: 200 dairy farmer loans, expand to crop farmer in 60 days

### MFI
- **Pain**: Field agent costs, group model doesn't scale to individual, no tech for voice/WhatsApp
- **Pitch**: "Reduce field agent dependency 60%, enable individual lending for graduated SHG members"
- **Pricing**: INR 50K-1L/mo + INR 100-150/loan
- **Pilot**: 300 SHG women in UP/Bihar

### Green NBFC
- **Pitch**: "Finance EV and solar for rural/semi-urban with asset-backed scoring and payout-linked repayment"
- **Pricing**: INR 2-3L/mo + INR 300-500/loan

### Fintech
- **Pitch**: "Plug our intelligence layer into your stack. We provide segment scoring, dynamic products, voice/WhatsApp origination, compliance. You provide capital."
- **Pricing**: INR 50-100/decision API + monthly platform fee

## 4. Pricing Models

| Model | Best For | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Per-decision | Fintech/API-first | INR 50-100/decision, 10K/mo = INR 5-10L |
| Per-loan | NBFCs, MFIs | INR 200-700/loan, 1K/mo = INR 2-7L |
| SaaS subscription | PSBs, large NBFCs | INR 1-5L/mo + per-loan |
| Revenue share | Small NBFCs | 1-3% of disbursement |
| PSL-gap premium | PSBs with shortfall | 2x rate for agriculture loans |
| FLDG-backed | Risk-averse | Platform takes 5% first-loss, charges premium |
