## 5. RBI REGULATORY FRAMEWORK: Priority Sector Lending (PSL)

The RBI mandates that a portion of all bank lending goes to underserved segments. This is the structural backbone that forces credit flow:

| PSL Category | Target (% of ANBC) | Sub-targets | Key Credit Products Mapped |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agriculture** | 18% | Small/marginal farmers: 10% of ANBC | KCC, NABARD refinance, Agri-Infra Fund |
| **Micro Enterprises** | 7.5% | - | PMMY, CGTMSE, PM SVANidhi, PM Vishwakarma |
| **Advances to Weaker Sections** | 12% | SHGs, SC/ST, women, minorities, PWD | Stand Up India, NRLM SHG-BLP, PMEGP (special category) |
| **Small Enterprises** | No separate sub-target; part of overall 40% | - | CGTMSE, TReDS, SIDBI refinance |
| **Overall PSL** | 40% (domestic banks); 75% (RRBs); 40% (foreign banks with 20+ branches) | - | All above schemes contribute to PSL compliance |

**Shortfall mechanism:** Banks not meeting PSL targets must place shortfall funds with SIDBI/NABARD/NHB at below-market rates (RIDF, MSME Fund, etc.), which these DFIs then deploy for the underserved. This creates a self-correcting loop.

## 7. CROSS-CUTTING THEMES

### 7A. Women-Focused Credit Enablement
- **PMMY:** 68% of all loans (by count) to women; Rs 16.33 lakh Cr disbursed to women
- **Stand Up India:** Mandates 1 woman entrepreneur per bank branch
- **SHG-BLP (NRLM):** Almost entirely women (9 Cr+ SHG members)
- **PM SVANidhi:** Significant women vendor participation
- **CGTMSE Export Credit:** 90% guarantee coverage for women-led MSMEs
- **Budget 2026:** First-time women, SC, ST entrepreneurs: special lending push

### 7B. SC/ST/Minority Inclusion
- **Stand Up India:** Mandatory SC/ST lending per branch
- **PMEGP:** Higher subsidy (25-35%) for special categories
- **PM Vishwakarma:** Traditional artisan trades (predominantly OBC/SC)
- **NRLM:** Specific minority/tribal sub-plans
- **PSL Weaker Section target:** 12% of ANBC mandated

### 7C. Financial Inclusion Foundation (JAM Trinity)
- **Jan Dhan (PMJDY):** 56+ Cr zero-balance accounts; Rs 2.68 lakh Cr deposits; provides bank account foundation for all DBT and credit schemes
- **Aadhaar:** 140 Cr+ enrollments; digital KYC backbone
- **Mobile:** 120 Cr+ smartphone users; UPI ecosystem

### 7D. Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) as Credit Adjacent
- Rs 3.48 lakh Cr leakage savings
- 315+ schemes deliver benefits via DBT into Jan Dhan accounts
- Creates transaction history and banking relationship that enables future credit access
- PM-KISAN (Rs 6,000/yr to 11 Cr+ farmers) directly builds bank account activity that KCC lenders can assess
